Together with the Green Council Group, I filed a council initiative in Kirkkonummi on 16 June 2025 to fly the Pride flag on the municipality's flagpoles every year. The council approved it on 9 March 2026 by 30 votes to 14. The flag costs the municipality very little, but for rainbow youth and other minorities it shows that Kirkkonummi stands behind the equal treatment its strategy promises.

## What does the initiative propose?

We proposed that Kirkkonummi fly the rainbow flag every year during the national Pride week. The case rests on the municipality's own strategy, which promises equality, equal treatment and a safe, youth-friendly municipality. In the school health survey, rainbow youth report more loneliness, depression and discrimination than their peers, and the flag is a visible message that the municipality is on their side.

## How did the council decide?

The council approved the initiative on 9 March 2026 by 30 votes to 14 after a lively debate. Kirkkonummi now flies the flag twice a year: during Helsinki Pride week and at the parish rainbow mass in September. I wrote about the vote and my speech in [Kirkkonummi is more equal today than yesterday](/en/blog/52/kirkkonummi-is-more-equal-today-than-yesterday/).

## The motion in full

*Filed with Kirkkonummi municipal council on 16 June 2025. Translated from the Finnish original.*

In Finland, the activities of municipalities are guided by the Local Government Act, which obliges municipalities to promote the wellbeing of their residents and to organise services in an economically, socially and environmentally sustainable manner. In Kirkkonummi, this work is guided by the Local Government Act as well as the municipal strategy, whose objectives include, among other things, promoting equality and equal treatment.

Rainbow youth report in the school health survey significantly more loneliness, depression and discrimination than their peers. According to its municipal strategy, Kirkkonummi is a safe and youth-friendly municipality. The municipality now has an opportunity to demonstrate that these goals are being realised in concrete actions. Approving Pride flag flying would be a clear message that Kirkkonummi is committed to promoting equality and combating discrimination. It would also strengthen the sense of community that the municipality aims for in its strategy.

Pride flag flying is already in use in many cities and municipalities throughout Finland. For example, Finland's most attractive city, Tampere, flies flags during Manse Pride week, and our neighbouring city Espoo is a partner of Helsinki Pride. Behind Pride is a broad network of local and national actors. For example, the Evangelical Lutheran Church of Finland and the Association of Finnish Local and Regional Authorities have been Pride partners.

We, the undersigned councillors, propose that Kirkkonummi municipality adopts annual Pride flag flying in the colours of the rainbow flag. The flag flying would take place on the municipality's flagpoles during the national Pride week. Pride flag flying is a small and low-cost gesture for the municipality, but its symbolic significance for minority residents and their loved ones is enormous.

The Green Council Group

Lauri Lavanti
