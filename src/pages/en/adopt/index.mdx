Adopt a sign and back my campaign with €30. It means a lot to me. As thanks, your name is written on the bottom edge of the sign, and the sign goes up by a roadside in Uusimaa in spring.

Finland has always been prepared. We keep grain, medicine and fuel in store for hard times, and icebreakers keep the sea lanes open in the harshest winter. A crisis used to cut the shipping routes. Now a single decision on another continent can be enough.

When the United States sanctioned the chief prosecutor of the International Criminal Court, he lost access to his email. The Ministry of Justice had already decided to move the election information system to Amazon's cloud and only reversed the decision in March 2026.

<Plate ground="offWhite" heading="How the donation works">
<PostProse>

Your name is written on the bottom edge of the sign, apart from the campaign text. Your name is visible to people who pass by on foot or by bike. If you like, your name also appears in the list of adopters on this page.

<details>
<summary>The donation, the limits and companies</summary>

The donation goes to the election fundraiser of my support association, Digitaalisen itsenäisyyden puolesta ry. The association pays for the signs, and your name on the sign is a thank-you for the donation. I can accept at most €6,000 from the same donor, counting every way of donating together. If your donations add up to at least €2,000, your name is reported in my campaign finance disclosure.

I cannot accept support from public bodies, companies controlled by a public body or foreign companies. A company can adopt a sign in its own name when the donation is paid from the company's account by invoice. The invoice has no VAT, and the donation is not tax-deductible. If you withdraw permission to use your name, we cover the name on the sign and remove it from the website.

</details>

I report my budget openly throughout the campaign. On the [campaign finance page](/en/elections/campaign-finance/) you can see my budget and what campaigns in Uusimaa cost in 2023.

</PostProse>
