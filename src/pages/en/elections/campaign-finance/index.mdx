<Plate eyebrow="Why" ground="sand" heading="Why publish the figures at all?">
    <Prose>
        <p>I demand openness about money in politics, so I start with my own campaign. The law requires a disclosure only after the election, and only from elected members and their alternates. I publish the figures during the campaign, whether or not I win a seat.</p>
        <p>Money is not a neutral instrument in politics. It shapes whose message is heard, and a large donation can create an expectation of something in return. So I name the source of every euro.</p>
        <p>I am also campaigning to change the law. Election funding should come from the people who have a vote, and every donor should be published. As long as corporate funding is open to every candidate, I will not limit myself alone. I disclose the money instead.</p>
        <p>My aim is that a voter can judge me through the money as well. If a figure is wrong, I will correct it at once: <a href="/en/contact/">get in touch</a>. There is more about the campaign on the <a href="/en/elections/">election page</a>.</p>
    </Prose>
</Plate>

The cheapest cost nothing and the most expensive 136,739 euros, and it is those few very expensive campaigns that pull the average above the median. That is why I compare my budget with the median. My own figure is a plan; the 2023 figures are money actually spent.</p>
