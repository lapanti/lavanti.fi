<Plate eyebrow="Why" ground="sand" heading="Why publish the figures at all?">
    <Prose>
        <p>I demand openness about money in politics, so I start with my own campaign. The law requires a disclosure only after the election, and only from members who are elected and their alternates. I publish the figures during the campaign, whether or not I win a seat.</p>
        <p>Money is not a neutral instrument in politics. It decides whose message is seen and heard, and a large donation inevitably creates an expectation of something in return. So I name the source of every euro and keep the campaign small: 30 000 euros is roughly what an average winning campaign cost in 2023.</p>
        <p>My aim is that a voter can judge me through the money as well. If a figure on this page is wrong, I will correct it at once — <a href="/en/contact/">get in touch</a>. There is more about the campaign on the <a href="/en/elections/">election page</a>.</p>
    </Prose>
</Plate>
