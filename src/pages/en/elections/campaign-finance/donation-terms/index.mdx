## Who receives the donation?

Donations are received by Lauri Lavanti's support association:

- Digitaalisen itsenäisyyden puolesta ry
- Business ID: 3632648-3
- Registered office: Kirkkonummi
- Postal address: c/o Marian Jecu, Pappilanmäki 3 F 23, 02400 Kirkkonummi, Finland
- Email: lauri@lavanti.fi

## What is the donation used for?

The association's purpose is to promote public debate, increase voter turnout in the parliamentary elections and support Lauri Lavanti's parliamentary election campaign. Donations are mainly used for campaign costs such as advertising, printed materials and events.

The association pays the costs directly from its own account. Expenses are approved by the association's board, and the operations auditor reviews the accounts. Any money left over is used for activities in line with the association's rules.

You can see the campaign's income and expenses on the [campaign finance page](/en/elections/campaign-finance/).

## What are the terms of the donation?

- You choose the amount yourself.
- A single donor can support a candidate in the parliamentary elections with at most 6,000 euros. This includes support given to the association and support given directly to the candidate.
- If your support totals 1,500 euros or more, your name and the amount are included in the election finance disclosure to the National Audit Office of Finland. The disclosures are public.
- Lauri Lavanti publishes every contribution of 2,000 euros or more with the donor's name during the campaign.
- We do not accept anonymous donations.
- Donations are not tax-deductible.
- We refund a donation if it was made by mistake or exceeds the legal limits.

## How do we handle donor data?

The controller of donor data is Digitaalisen itsenäisyyden puolesta ry. We store your name, your phone number and the amount and time of the donation. We receive the data from MobilePay when you donate.

We use the data for the association's bookkeeping and for disclosures required by the Act on a Candidate's Election Funding. A donation reveals your political views, so we use the data only for these purposes. The data is seen by the association's board and by Lauri Lavanti, who files the election finance disclosure. We do not share the data with anyone else unless the law requires it. We do not use the data for marketing.

We keep the data for as long as the Accounting Act requires, which is six years from the end of the financial year.

MobilePay processes the payment data under its own terms.

You have the right to access your data and to ask for incorrect data to be corrected. More information on your rights is available from the [Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/).

## How do I give feedback or make a complaint?

Send feedback or complaints by email to lauri@lavanti.fi. We reply within two weeks.

If you believe the processing of your data breaches data protection law, you can [lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/).
