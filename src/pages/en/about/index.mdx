    <Eyebrow>Background</Eyebrow>
    <Prose>
        <p>He is <a href="/en/elections/">the Greens' parliamentary candidate in the Uusimaa electoral district in the 2027 elections</a>, a lead developer, municipal councillor in Kirkkonummi, and chair of the Greens council group. He has lived in Kirkkonummi since the age of three and attended comprehensive school, upper secondary school, and vocational school in his home municipality. He graduated as a Master of Science (Tech.) from Aalto University and focuses on AI, economic policy, <a href="/en/category/entrepreneurship/">entrepreneurship</a>, and digital independence. Digital independence, privacy, and the responsible adoption of AI are all part of the same whole: how we build technological development that is both competitive and sustainable.</p>
        <p>Lauri wrote more about his candidacy in its <a href="/en/blog/67/my-candidacy-for-parliament/">announcement</a>. Lauri also has a <a href="https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauri_Lavanti">Wikipedia article</a> (in Finnish).</p>

    <p class="answer" slot="answer">I am the initiator and primary contact of the Digital Independence citizen initiative. Our initiative calls for legislation ensuring that critical public digital services and data remain in the hands of Finnish and European actors, on servers located in the EU/EEA area. The initiative collected the 50,000 signatures required for parliamentary consideration in July 2026.</p>
    <p class="detail" slot="detail">Digital independence supports European technological development, reduces dependence on individual vendors, and creates better conditions for domestic software development. It is not an ideology but a practical prerequisite for a functioning society — and it also strengthens democracy and citizens' rights, while creating markets for new business activity. In my work I am guided by justice, equality, and democracy: on my initiative, Kirkkonummi flies the rainbow flag twice a year.</p>
</Pillar>

The program is based on information technology, but it also covers other subjects such as aesthetics and philosophy. The program has taught me to view systems as both technical and humane entities.</p>
        <p>My multidisciplinary background has given me the ability to act smoothly as a bridge builder, fostering mutual understanding between different professional groups. I also always examine technology from the perspective of its significance, impact, and responsibility. In practice, that means I can speak with both technical experts and business representatives — and make sure they understand each other.</p>
