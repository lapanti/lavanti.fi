<IntroPlate eyebrow="Parliamentary election 2027" heading="Why am I running?">
<VideoEmbed lang="en" posterImage="Lauri-Lavanti-vaalivideo" slot="video" title="Why am I running? (in Finnish)" videoId="fiLEp7wLK3I" />
    <p>For decades, Finland has been a model country for high technology and expertise — from Vaisala to icebreakers, Nokia to Linux, Supercell to ICEYE and IQM. That position is now wavering. At the same time, AI is fundamentally reshaping society: it is changing work, trust in digital services, and how people connect with each other. On top of that, the state's economic situation limits what we can do.</p>
    <p>Now, more than ever, we need expertise and understanding of technology, AI, and how they affect society and markets. I have a technology degree from Aalto University and over a decade of hands-on experience building secure software and banking services. I understand the different ways technology can be put to use and how it affects society. I also understand where its limits must be set, so that we uphold the rule of law.</p>
    <p>Some of these limits are especially valuable: they protect all of our privacy and fundamental rights. Privacy protection is not a brake on progress — it is a precondition for democracy.</p>
    <p>I am Lauri Lavanti — a Kirkkonummi municipal councillor, chair of the Green council group, father of four, lead developer, and Master of Science in Technology. On these pages you'll find my thoughts on the future and what lies ahead for us. Practical details about the candidacy and voting are on the <a href="/en/elections/">election page</a>.</p>

<Synthesis eyebrow="Synthesis" heading="Digital independence">
    <p>Finland and Europe need to control their own digital infrastructure. That's why I launched the Digital Independence citizens' initiative, which collected the 50,000 signatures required for parliamentary consideration in July 2026. In the initiative, we demand that Finland reduce its dependence on foreign digital service providers and strengthen digital sovereignty.</p>
    <p>Finland's public administration is heavily dependent on, for example, major American cloud providers, the so-called hyperscalers — Amazon, Microsoft, and Google. Since 2022, it has become uncomfortably clear that geopolitical power games are here to stay, including in the digital operating environment. Finnish and European alternatives exist: UpCloud is a concrete example of a high-performance domestic cloud service that the public sector can use. I advocate for breaking up public IT procurements, favouring open source, and factoring crisis preparedness — digital security of supply — into procurement decisions.</p>
</Synthesis>
